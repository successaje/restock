Pay your installment

Paid in cNGN, a naira stablecoin on Celo. 1 cNGN = ₦1.

How it works

  1. You pay in cNGN from your own wallet, the same naira amount as always.
  2. Your installer's agent receives it and protects part of it in dollars, so they can afford to restock the panels and batteries you depend on.
  3. Your wallet needs a little CELO or USD₮ for the network fee.
